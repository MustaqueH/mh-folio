Endpoint Analytics & Auto-Remediation

Using telemetry and automation to detect issues early, reduce noise, and improve device health at scale.

Automation & Insights

From reactive tickets to proactive fixes

Transforming raw endpoint signals into playbooks that automatically resolve or prevent incidents.

Rather than waiting for users to log tickets, I use Endpoint Analytics and Intune reporting to surface slow boot times, app failures, and configuration drifts. These signals feed PowerShell- and policy-based remediation that quietly correct issues before they become widespread problems.

Objectives

  • Identify recurring endpoint issues using analytics and telemetry.
  • Build auto-remediation playbooks for high-volume, low-complexity issues.
  • Reduce incident volume and improve end-user experience measurably.

Responsibilities

  • Configured Endpoint Analytics and Intune reporting to surface performance and reliability signals.
  • Designed remediation scripts (PowerShell) triggered via Intune or scheduled tasks.
  • Aligned thresholds and playbooks with support teams to avoid conflicting actions.
  • Reported on trends and outcomes to stakeholders across IT and the business.

Technologies & Tools

  • Endpoint Analytics
  • Microsoft Intune
  • Proactive Remediations
  • PowerShell
  • Azure Monitor

Impact & Outcomes

  • Reduced endpoint tickets for known issues through automated remediation.
  • Improved device health scores and boot performance across targeted groups.
  • Freed up engineering and support time previously spent on repetitive fixes.
