Endpoint Analytics & auto-remediation

Use device health signals to fix repeat problems before they become tickets—and prove the estate is trending the right way.

Automation & insights

From telemetry to action

Proactive remediations and analytics views that shrink incident volume and mean time to repair.

Endpoint Analytics highlights where the fleet is actually struggling—startup performance, app reliability, policy failures—before users flood the service desk. I pair those signals with scripted remediations and Intune-native actions so common break/fix patterns clear automatically, with guardrails and reporting so operations stays in control.

Objectives

  • Reduce repeat incidents driven by the same misconfiguration or stale state.
  • Give stakeholders a credible story on device health—not just green/red dashboards.
  • Keep automation auditable: what ran, on whom, and what changed.

Responsibilities

  • Defined health baselines and “fix first” remediations aligned to top failure modes.
  • Built and tested proactive remediation scripts with rollback and targeting rings.
  • Worked with service desk on triage playbooks so human steps complement automation.

Technologies & tools

  • Microsoft Intune
  • Endpoint Analytics
  • Proactive remediations
  • PowerShell
  • Windows health reports

Impact & outcomes

  • Fewer tickets for recurring endpoint issues once remediations covered the top buckets.
  • Faster mean time to repair when automation handled safe fixes without a desk touch.
  • Clearer visibility for IT leads on which cohorts still need investment.
