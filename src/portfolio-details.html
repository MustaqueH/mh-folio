Enterprise Intune App Lifecycle Management

End-to-end application packaging, deployment, and lifecycle automation for enterprise endpoints

Lead architect for application lifecycle at scale — I package, validate, and automate deployment of business-critical software across a co-managed estate. Solutions combine Win32 packaging (.intunewin), PowerShell detection logic, Graph API automation, and deployment orchestration to deliver reliable installs and near-zero disruption for end users.

Objectives

  • Deliver reliable, repeatable application installs with clear rollback paths.
  • Automate packaging and deployment to reduce human error and manual effort.
  • Improve deployment success rates and reduce helpdesk volume.

Responsibilities

  • Packaged and deployed 170+ Win32 apps (.intunewin) with robust detection and rollback.
  • Authored reusable PowerShell modules and deployment templates for scale.
  • Built monitoring dashboards surfacing failures and automated remediation triggers.

Impact & Outcomes

  • 98%+ successful deployments across managed devices.
  • 80% reduction in software-related helpdesk tickets.
  • Faster rollouts with minimal end-user interruption.
