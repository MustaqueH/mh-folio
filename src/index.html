Frequently Asked Questions

I believe genuine connections build the best collaborations. Here are answers to questions that help you get to know me both as an IT specialist and as a person, including what motivates me and what I value beyond technology.

What kind of problems can you help me solve?

I specialize in resolving technical issues, streamlining IT workflows, managing devices through Intune, and optimizing Microsoft 365 environments. While I'm not a front-end developer or web designer by title, I do offer practical services like building websites, designing logos, and helping individuals or small businesses get started online. Whether it's fixing a system bug or bringing a digital idea to life, I'm here to support your goals.

I'm not tech-savvy—can I still reach out to you?

Absolutely. You don't need to speak in code! I keep things simple and approachable - whether you're dealing with a tech issue, exploring a career in IT, or just curious about where to begin. I'm always happy to share what I've learned, offer career guidance, or even connect you with people across different fields - not just tech. Sometimes, a good conversation is all it takes to spark the next step.

What's your general approach when taking on a new task or project?

I always start by listening - because understanding your needs is the key to solving the right problem. From there, I analyze, prioritize, and move forward with clarity and calm. My approach is people-first, solution-focused, and rooted in the belief that great outcomes come from thoughtful collaboration. Whether it's resolving an issue or building something from scratch, I'm here to simplify the complex, empower progress, and make the journey feel less overwhelming and more impactful.

Can I connect with you just to brainstorm or explore an idea?

You don't need a big project or a pressing problem to reach out. Whether you're looking to collaborate, exchange ideas, get career advice, or simply talk tech or creativity - I'm always open to a good conversation. Sometimes, the best ideas start over a casual chat.

What drives your passion about working with Technology today?

What drives my passion is how rapidly technology is evolving - and the opportunity it gives us to build better, smarter ways of working and living. Today, keeping up with tech isn't just for IT professionals; it's essential for everyone. I see my role as a bridge between complex systems and real-world needs - making tech more accessible, useful, and empowering for people from all walks of life. It's about progress, connection, and staying ready for what's next.

What do you enjoy doing when you're not working with Technology?

When I'm not working with technology, I'm all about staying active and creative. Fitness is a big part of my routine, and I love playing cricket whenever I get the chance. I'm also a passionate PC gamer - it's my way to unwind and challenge myself. On the creative side, photography and videography let me capture moments and tell stories beyond the screen. These hobbies keep me balanced and inspired in both life and work.
