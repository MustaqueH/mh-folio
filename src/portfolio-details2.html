System & Endpoint Projects

Enterprise Endpoint & Automation Initiatives

Practical projects improving provisioning, compliance, and operational efficiency

These projects focus on modernising device lifecycle management, automating routine tasks, and improving incident resolution across enterprise endpoints. Work spans Intune configuration, Autopilot provisioning, Win32 packaging, PowerShell automation, and monitoring integrations.

Objectives

  • Reduce device provisioning time and manual steps (target <30 minutes).
  • Improve deployment success and reduce app-related incidents.
  • Automate repetitive admin tasks to free engineering time.

Responsibilities / Deliverables

  • Packaged and deployed 170+ Win32 apps (.intunewin) with detection logic.
  • Built Autopilot profiles and device enrollment flows to enable zero-touch provisioning.
  • Authored PowerShell modules for automated onboarding, compliance checks, and patch orchestration.
  • Integrated Intune telemetry with monitoring dashboards for proactive remediation.
  • Standardised baseline images and configuration profiles across OS versions.

Technologies & Tools

  • Microsoft Intune
  • Autopilot
  • PowerShell
  • Microsoft Graph
  • SCCM / Co-management
  • Azure Monitor

Impact & Outcomes

  • Provisioning time reduced to under 30 minutes for standard laptops.
  • Deployment success rate improved to 98%+ across managed apps.
  • Helpdesk tickets from app installs reduced by 70% due to robust packaging and detection.
  • Saved engineering hours via automated onboarding and remediation scripts.
